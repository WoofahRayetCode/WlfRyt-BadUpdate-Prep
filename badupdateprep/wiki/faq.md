# FAQ

From grimdoomer’s README.

Q: Why do I re-run this every time I turn the console on?
A: It is not persistent. Off or reboot = stock again. There will not be a software-only boot-to-hacked-state “softmod”.

Q: Should I use this instead of RGH?
A: No. No soldering, but inferior to RGH in every other way. Treat it as a proof of concept unless you cannot open the console.

Q: Winchester?
A: Yes.

Q: Original Xbox Tony Hawk Wasteland?
A: No. Only the 360 version, and v1.3 dropped even that in favor of Rock Band Blitz.

Q: Other skate/music games?
A: No. The save exploits are game-specific.

Q: Why only one unsigned xex?
A: The exploit patches the hypervisor and launches default.xex. XeUnshackle/FreeMyXe are the usual payloads for a usable session (plugins, homebrew).

Q: Xbox Live?
A: Disconnect. Presence of CFW is not the ban trigger; going online during/after unsigned code can be. This app cannot make Live safe.

Q: NAND backup?
A: Dump with the **read-only** flasher if you included it. Do not write NAND on a Bad Update console — that can brick.
