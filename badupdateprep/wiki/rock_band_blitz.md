# Rock Band Blitz (DaTArrest)

The “DaTArrest” save exploit by ihatecompvir and Emma (InvoxiPlayGames) launches Bad Update.

Works on the **arcade trial** and the **full game**.

WARNING: If you have Rock Band 1/2/3 DLC or custom songs, move them to another drive and **disconnect that drive** before running the exploit.

## While it runs

The menu stays animated and music plays. If the menu or music **stops**, the run failed — reboot and try again.

## Freeze: immediately at “Discovering Downloadable Content”

Retry. Wait a few seconds before pressing A.

## Freeze: progress bar partially fills at “Discovering Downloadable Content”

Rock Band DLC/customs are probably installed. Move RB1/2/3 DLC off this console/drive and disconnect that storage.

## Freeze: immediately at “Running exploit…”

Use **one** USB only. Root must have BadUpdatePayload with the Blitz exploit bins from the official pack (this app copies them).

## Freeze: after the ring of light changes color

Bad Update itself failed. Power off and retry. Check USB files if it keeps failing.

## Goes straight to the main menu (no exploit)

The save did not load. Delete “Rock Band Blitz Library File” if present. Confirm the exploit save is under Content/ on the USB this app built.

## Boot animation recovery (v1.3)

On “Press A To Start”, **hold Y and press A** (keep holding Y). Uses the stock default.xex from the pack if you chose recovery payload in this app.
