# Bad Update — local guide

Adapted from grimdoomer's Xbox360BadUpdate wiki and README.
This copy lives inside the app so you do not need a browser.

Bad Update is a **non-persistent** software-only hypervisor exploit for Xbox 360 dashboard **2.0.17559.0**. Unsigned code runs only until you power off or reboot. It is not a softmod and is not a replacement for RGH.

v1.3 (the pack this app downloads) is Rock Band Blitz only. Tony Hawk's American Wasteland was dropped.

## What you need

- Xbox 360 on 17559 (any model, including Winchester)
- FAT32 USB (512 MB+ for the Blitz trial)
- Rock Band Blitz arcade trial (this app can download it) or the full game
- Stay **off Xbox Live** (unplug Ethernet, forget Wi-Fi)

- Official Retail USB pack (save exploit + BadUpdatePayload)
- XeUnshackle or FreeMyXe as BadUpdatePayload/default.xex

You still launch the game **on the console**.
